How to use this

Rules (frozen taxonomy, conf/theme-taxonomy.json):

  • Label a theme only when the review complains about it. Praise is never labelled; a neutral mention is not a complaint.
  • Every labelled theme carries an exact contiguous quote from the title or the text, at most 15 words. Never paraphrase or repair.
  • Themes the review does not complain about are omitted, not marked absent.
  • Other is a complaint fitting none of the ten, with a phrase of at most 5 words.
  • Abstain means the review cannot be labelled at all — it carries no themes and no other, and its confidence is forced to low.

Keys: 1–9, 0 toggle themes 1–10 · ← → prev/next review · o other · / abstain · u use selection for the last theme you turned on.
