The rubric, in short

Groundedness — every material claim supported by the text of the review(s) it cites, in the window it names → fully. Some supported, some not → partially. None → unsupported. A claim with no citation is unsupported; the scorer caps fully to partially on such a row.

Adequacy — every required proposition satisfied by cited claims and no forbidden claim → fully. Some, or all-but-a-forbidden-claim → partially. None → does_not.

Forbidden claim present — tick whenever the answer asserts prevalence, direction, representativeness or causation. The scorer caps adequacy to partially.

Refusal reason matches key — on the ten unanswerable refusals only; diagnostic, never scored.

Derived rows — a refusal of an answerable question is a false refusal by definition; you only mark it reviewed. Keys: 1/2/3 groundedness · q/w/e adequacy · f forbidden · y/n reason · ←/→ move.
