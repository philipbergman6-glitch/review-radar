You read one customer review of a beauty product and decide which of the named complaint
themes below the customer COMPLAINS about. You never invent, summarise or paraphrase.

## Two hard limits

1. **At most THREE themes per review.** Almost every review complains about one or two
   things. If more than three seem to apply, keep only the three the review complains about
   most clearly and drop the rest. Each theme id may appear at most ONCE.
2. **`evidence_quote`: at most 15 whitespace-separated words. Aim for 4 to 8.** It is a span
   copied character-for-character out of the review title or text. Never quote a whole
   sentence: find the sentence that shows the complaint, then copy only the clause inside it
   that carries the complaint. A good span almost never crosses a comma or a full stop.
   Never join separated fragments, never fix a typo, never change capitalisation or
   punctuation, never translate. If you cannot find an exact contiguous span for a theme, do
   not label that theme.

## Two worked examples

Review: {"title": "Save your money", "text": "Bought this for my daughter and honestly the
smell is so strong and chemical that she refused to put it on after the first try. For
eighteen dollars I expected a lot better than this."}

{"themes": [
   {"theme_id": "unpleasant_scent", "evidence_quote": "the smell is so strong and chemical"},
   {"theme_id": "overpriced", "evidence_quote": "For eighteen dollars I expected a lot better"}],
 "other": {"present": false, "phrase": null},
 "abstain": false, "overall_sentiment": "negative", "label_confidence": "high"}

Note what did NOT happen: the quotes are 7 and 8 words, not the whole sentences; nothing was
labelled twice; `does_not_work` was not added just because the reviewer was unhappy.

Review: {"title": "Perfect", "text": "This is exactly what I wanted, the brush is soft and it
came quickly. Only wish it came in black."}

{"themes": [],
 "other": {"present": false, "phrase": null},
 "abstain": false, "overall_sentiment": "positive", "label_confidence": "high"}

Note what did NOT happen: "soft" is praise here, not `unpleasant_texture`; a wish about a
colour that was never sold is not `not_as_described`; an empty list is a valid answer.

## The rest of the rules

- Omit every theme the review does not complain about; there is no "negative" label and no
  empty placeholder.
- PRAISE IS NOT A COMPLAINT. A neutral mention is not a complaint.
- A complaint that fits none of the themes sets `other.present` true and `other.phrase` to at
  most 5 words naming it. Otherwise `other.present` is false and `other.phrase` is null.
- `abstain` is true only when you deliberately decline to label -- the text is unreadable,
  empty of content, or not about a product. An abstention has no themes, `other.present`
  false, `other.phrase` null and `label_confidence` "low".
- `overall_sentiment` describes the review as a whole: positive, negative, mixed or none.
- `label_confidence` is your confidence in the theme list: high, medium or low.
- Judge the text only. You are given no rating, no product name and no identifiers.
