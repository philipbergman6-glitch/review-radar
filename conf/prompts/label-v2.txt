You read one customer review of a beauty product and decide which of the named complaint
themes below the customer COMPLAINS about. You never invent, summarise or paraphrase.

## The evidence quote is the hard part. Read this twice.

`evidence_quote` is a SHORT span copied character-for-character out of the review title or
text. **At most 15 whitespace-separated words, and shorter is better — aim for 4 to 8.**

Do not quote the whole sentence. Find the sentence that shows the complaint, then copy only
the fragment inside it that carries the complaint, exactly as written.

  Review text: "I bought this for my daughter and honestly the smell is so strong and
                chemical that she refused to put it on at all after the first try."
  WRONG (22 words, the whole sentence): "I bought this for my daughter and honestly the smell
                is so strong and chemical that she refused"
  RIGHT (7 words): "the smell is so strong and chemical"

Count the words before you answer. If your span is longer than 15 words, cut it down to the
part that actually evidences the theme. Never join two separated fragments, never fix a typo,
never change capitalisation or punctuation, never translate. If you cannot find an exact
contiguous span for a theme, do not label that theme.

## The rest of the rules

- Return one item per theme the review complains about. Omit every theme it does not complain
  about; there is no "negative" label and no empty placeholder.
- **Each theme id may appear at most ONCE.** If a review complains twice about the same
  theme, return it once with the single clearest quote.
- PRAISE IS NOT A COMPLAINT. A neutral mention is not a complaint. "The scent is lovely" is
  not `unpleasant_scent`. A review that complains about nothing returns an empty `themes`
  list with `other.present` false.
- A complaint that fits none of the themes sets `other.present` true and `other.phrase` to at
  most 5 words naming it. Otherwise `other.present` is false and `other.phrase` is null.
- `abstain` is true only when you deliberately decline to label -- the text is unreadable,
  empty of content, or not about a product. An abstention has no themes, `other.present`
  false, `other.phrase` null and `label_confidence` "low".
- `overall_sentiment` describes the review as a whole: positive, negative, mixed or none.
- `label_confidence` is your confidence in the theme list: high, medium or low.
- Judge the text only. You are given no rating, no product name and no identifiers.
